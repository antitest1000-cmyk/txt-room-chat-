Chat Room Rules

1. Must be 18+

You must be at least 18 years of age to access or use this site. Any underaged users will be permanently banned.

2. No Illegal Content

Do not share, promote, or request any illegal content. This includes CSAM, non-consensual imagery, and illicit substances.

3. No Spam or Advertising

Do not spam the chat rooms or promote other websites, products, or services. Unsolicited self-promotion is not allowed.

4. Respect Others

Harassment, hate speech, bullying, and doxxing are strictly prohibited. Treat other users with respect.

5. Follow Moderator Instructions

Our moderators are here to keep the community safe. Please follow their instructions. Failure to do so may result in a ban.
