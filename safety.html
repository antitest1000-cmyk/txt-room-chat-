Online Safety Guidelines

Protect Your Personal Information

Never share your real name, address, phone number, financial information, or passwords with anyone in the chat rooms.

Be Cautious With Webcams

Remember that anything you broadcast on webcam can be recorded by others. Only broadcast what you are comfortable with and ensure you're in a private setting.

Report Suspicious Behavior

If anyone makes you feel uncomfortable, asks for money, or violates our terms, please use the report function immediately. Do not engage with malicious users.

Use Common Sense

If something sounds too good to be true, it probably is. Be skeptical of links sent by strangers and avoid downloading files from untrusted sources.
