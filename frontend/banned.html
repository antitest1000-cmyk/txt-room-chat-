Ban Information

🚫 Access Denied / Banned

If you are seeing this page, it means you have been suspended or banned from the Caught Me chat rooms due to a violation of our Terms of Use.

Common reasons for a ban include:
- Underage usage (Immediate permanent ban)
- Harassment, threats, or severe abuse
- Spamming, advertising, or selling services
- Broadcasting illegal or non-consensual content

Most minor chat bans are temporary and expire automatically within 24-48 hours. If you believe your ban was a mistake, or if a permanent ban was issued in error, you may submit a ban appeal via our Contact Page.
