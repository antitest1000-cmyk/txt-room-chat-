18 U.S.C. 2257 Record-Keeping Requirements Compliance Statement

All models, actors, actresses and other persons that appear in any visual depiction of actual or simulated sexually explicit conduct appearing on or otherwise contained in this Website were over the age of eighteen (18) years at the time the visual depiction was created.

Caught Me is a user-generated content platform. The owners and operators of Caught Me do not produce, direct, or control the creation of the visual depictions uploaded or broadcast by users.

With respect to all visual depictions uploaded or broadcast by users, Caught Me qualifies for the exemption provided under 18 U.S.C. § 2257(h)(2)(B)(v) and 28 C.F.R. § 75.1(c)(3) as a provider of an interactive computer service (as defined in 47 U.S.C. § 230(f)(2)) or an information location tool.

All users broadcasting or uploading visual depictions to this Website are required to confirm that they are at least 18 years of age and that all individuals appearing in their content are at least 18 years of age. Any user found violating these terms will be immediately and permanently banned, and their content will be removed.

Reporting Violations

If you believe that any content on this site involves a minor or violates 18 U.S.C. 2257, please immediately report it to our moderation team or contact the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) at 1-800-THE-LOST or via their CyberTipline at www.cybertipline.com.
