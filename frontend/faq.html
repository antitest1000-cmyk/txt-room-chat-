Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a chat room?

You can join any chat room by selecting it from the home page or the rooms list. You can join as a Guest by simply providing a nickname and your age, or you can register an account for a more permanent identity.

How do I use the live webcam?

Inside the cam-enabled chat rooms, you will see a camera icon in your controls. Click it, grant your browser permission to access your webcam and microphone, and you'll go live instantly.

How do I report a user?

If someone is breaking the rules, click on their avatar in the chat list and select the "Report" or "Ignore" option. Our moderators review reports regularly.

Can I change my username?

If you are a guest, you can simply leave and rejoin with a new nickname. Registered members cannot change their usernames once created.

Are the chat rooms moderated?

Yes, we have a mix of automated filters and human moderators who keep the rooms safe and enforce our Terms of Use.
