Become a Moderator

🛡️ Join Our Moderation Team

Caught Me relies on trusted community members to help keep our chat rooms safe, fun, and free of spam. We are always looking for active, level-headed users to join our moderation team.

What does a Moderator do?

  • Review and handle user reports.
  • Kick or temporarily mute users who are disrupting the chat.
  • Enforce the Terms of Use, especially concerning spam and harassment.
  • Foster a welcoming environment for new users.

Requirements

To be considered, you must have a registered account in good standing, be active on the platform for at least 3 months, and demonstrate maturity in the public chat rooms.

How to Apply

If you meet the requirements and wish to volunteer, please reach out to us via the Contact Page and include your username, how long you've been using the site, and why you'd be a good fit.
